Photo Booth Pro on your device. Photo Booth Pro turns the phone, tablet or PC you already own into a photo booth: live filters you can see before you shoot, four-shot strips, boomerangs and video, printed or shared on the spot.

If something here does not answer your question, write to us — the address is at the bottom, and a real person reads it.

This help center serves iPhone, iPad, Android and Windows. Features and settings differ by platform and version; Apple-specific instructions are not a promise of identical Android or Windows behavior.

The question everyone asks: mirroring

Why does my saved photo look flipped compared to the preview?

Because it is, on purpose, and you can change it.

On iPhone, iPad and Android, the front-camera preview is mirrored, like a real mirror, so that when you tilt your head left the image tilts left and you can actually pose. What gets saved is a separate choice:

Settings ▸ Capture ▸ Mirror Front Camera

  • Off (the default) — captures are flipped back to the way the room saw you. Writing on a T-shirt or a sign reads the right way round.
  • On — captures match the preview exactly.

The rear camera is never mirrored either way.

Getting started

Where do my photos go? iPhone and iPad: To your Photos library, in an album named in Settings ▸ Photos ▸ Album, and to the app's own Gallery tab.

Android: Captures are saved through MediaStore to your shared gallery, in the Photo Booth Pro album or the album you choose. They also appear in the app's Gallery. The app does not upload captures; your gallery's own backup settings are separate. Uninstalling does not remove these shared captures — delete them in your gallery if you want them gone.

How do I take a strip? Choose Strip on the mode selector. The booth takes four photos with a countdown between them, then opens the strip builder, where you can change the layout, the styling and which photos are used.

Can I build a strip from photos I already have? iPhone and iPad: In the Gallery, select up to four photos and choose Build strip, or import from your library in the strip builder.

Android: In Gallery, select four captures and choose Make a strip, or use the system photo picker in Strip Studio.

How long can a video be? iPhone and iPad: Up to five minutes. Recording also stops early if storage runs low or the device gets too warm, and the clip is kept either way rather than discarded.

What is Boomerang? A short clip that plays forward and then backward. Settings ▸ Capture ▸ Boomerang style switches between that and a plain forward loop.

Filters

How do I find a filter? Tap the filter button to open the rail, then search by name or browse by category. Tap the star to keep a favourite at the front.

Can I adjust how strong a filter is? Yes, where a filter has a strength. The control appears under the rail only for filters that have one, so there is never a row that does nothing.

Do filters affect the saved photo, or only the preview? Both. What you see is what gets saved — the filter runs on the live preview and on the capture through the same pipeline.

Permissions

The app is asking for the camera / microphone / Photos.

  • Camera — required. Without it there is no booth.
  • Microphone — only for video sound. Say no and video still records, in silence, and the booth tells you it is doing so. There is no setting for this inside the app: video always records sound when the microphone is allowed, because a silent clip is almost always a mistake rather than a choice. If you want video without sound, decline the microphone in the system settings.
  • Photo library on iPhone and iPad — needed to save captures to an album and to show your saved captures in the Gallery. Decline and captures stay inside the app.
  • Media access on Android — supports reading captures in Gallery. Saving uses shared MediaStore storage, not an app-private fallback. The system photo picker lets you choose existing pictures without granting broad library access.

I said no and changed my mind. Open your device's system settings, find Photo Booth Pro, and switch the permission on. The app picks it up when you come back.

Android: Use Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Photo Booth Pro ▸ Permissions. If Android no longer shows the permission prompt after repeated refusals, use the app's Open settings button.

My Gallery is empty and I know I took photos. iPhone and iPad: Usually this is limited photo library access: the app can only see the photos you picked. The Gallery says so when that is the case. Grant full access in your device's system settings, or tap the prompt in the Gallery to select more.

Android: Check media permissions and the selected album. Check your device's gallery as well: saved shared captures can remain there even if the app cannot currently read them.

Does Android use the internet? Yes. Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics send diagnostics and usage events by default, not your pictures or recordings. To opt out, turn off Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Share diagnostics. Capture and local editing do not need an online account. See the privacy policy for the collected data and deletion options.

Printing and sharing

Printing on iPhone and iPad uses AirPrint. Any AirPrint printer on the same Wi-Fi network will appear; there is nothing to install. Strips print at the aspect ratio of the layout you chose.

Android: Open a still photo or strip and choose Print. Android's print system may need your printer manufacturer's print service plugin. If no printer appears, check your device's Settings ▸ Connected devices ▸ Printing (the path varies by device). Windows: Set up the printer in Windows system settings.

Sharing uses your device's share menu, so anywhere you can send a photo from your photo library, you can send one from here — messaging apps, email, or any other app you choose.

Purchases

Android v1: All features are unlocked. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions or 48-hour event passes. There is no purchase to restore. The paid-feature guidance below describes iPhone and iPad, not Android v1.

iPhone and iPad purchases

What is free? Taking photos, strips, boomerangs and video; the Gallery; saving, sharing and printing; and a substantial part of the filter catalog.

What do the purchases unlock? The full filter catalog, removing the watermark from strips, all strip layouts, GIF and video export, and full-resolution export. There is also a 48-hour event pass for a single occasion.

I bought it and it is not unlocked. Settings ▸ Your plan ▸ Restore purchases. If that does not do it, make sure you are signed in with the account you bought with, then write to us.

I bought it on one device — do I have to buy it again on another? The iPhone and iPad versions share one App Store record; one purchase covers both on the same Apple Account. For Windows purchases, use the Microsoft account you bought with and check the store's terms for device eligibility. Purchases do not carry over between different app stores — for example, from an Apple Account to a Google account — since each store handles its own billing.

Refunds are handled by the store you purchased from: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google Play at support.google.com/googleplay, or Microsoft at support.microsoft.com.

Privacy

The app does not upload your photos or videos to us and has no login. Sharing and printing use your device's system services; gallery backup is controlled by your own backup settings.

On Android, Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics send crash reports, app interactions, device diagnostics, installation identifiers and approximate location derived from IP, on by default. This is not anonymous aggregate-only data. No media, audio, camera frames, guest captions or media filenames are included. Android has no face detection, ads, advertising-ID access or location permission.

On Android, iPhone and iPad, you can switch off app diagnostic collection in Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Share diagnostics. The full detail — exactly what is collected, what is never collected, and why — is in the privacy policy.

Opting out or uninstalling does not erase previously collected server diagnostics. For deletion requests, email photoboothpro@outlook.com; installation-keyed records may not be identifiable from your email alone. See retention and deletion for the limits and process.

Accessibility

The app supports your device's screen reader, larger text sizes, reduced motion, and increased contrast settings. If you find a control that is unreachable, unlabelled or clipped at a large text size, please tell us — that is a defect and we will fix it.

Something is wrong

Before writing, it helps to know:

  • your device and its OS version (found in your device's system settings),
  • the app version (Settings ▸ About in Photo Booth Pro),
  • what you did, what happened, and what you expected instead.

Android: The in-app Share diagnostics switch controls Firebase crash reporting independently of the device's system diagnostic-sharing setting. You can still email for support if it is off.

iPhone and iPad: Apple's separate diagnostic-sharing option is in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Analytics & Improvements. Windows: System diagnostic sharing is managed in Windows privacy settings. These system controls do not replace the Android in-app switch.
